MindWell

Daily mood check-ins and a private journal

Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026

MindWell is a personal project, not a company or a professional service. This page is a plain description of how the app works, not a legal document.

MindWell is a personal mood journal. You write about how you're feeling, which is sensitive, so this page explains exactly what MindWell stores, where it goes, and how to delete it.

What MindWell collects

  • Your email address and password, to create your account. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service). MindWell never sees or stores your password in readable form.
  • Your check-ins. For each day you check in: the mood you picked (Calm, Okay, Anxious or Overwhelmed), the reflection you wrote, and the time you saved it.
  • Companion messages. When you use the Companion, your messages and your check-ins from the last 14 days are sent off to generate a reply (details below). MindWell doesn't save these conversations. They're gone when you leave or clear the page.

MindWell doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Firebase keeps you signed in using your browser's local storage.

Where it's stored and who handles it

  • Google Firebase stores your account (Firebase Authentication) and your check-ins (Cloud Firestore). The database rules only let your signed-in account read or write your own entries.
  • Cloudflare runs a small server that checks you're signed in and passes Companion messages along. It doesn't save message content.
  • Groq, an AI provider, receives Companion messages and generates the replies. It handles them under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve the fonts and the chart library. Like any website request, they can see your IP address.

How it's used

Only to run the app: drawing your calendar, journal and insights, and giving the Companion some context about your recent days. MindWell doesn't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

The person who runs MindWell has administrator access to the database and could technically view stored data. Entries are not looked at unless you ask for help with a problem that requires it, or the law requires it.

Deleting your data

  • One entry: on the Home page, choose the day on the calendar, then select "Delete this entry".
  • Everything: on the Journal page, select "Delete my account and data". This permanently removes every check-in and then your account. It can't be undone.

MindWell is not medical care

MindWell is a self-reflection tool. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and it isn't a substitute for a doctor, therapist or counselor. No one monitors what you write. If you write that you're in danger, nobody will be alerted. If you need help now, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.

Children

MindWell isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
